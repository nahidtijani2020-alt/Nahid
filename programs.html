Our programs

Four ways we help children and families, and how your gift can reach them.

Education

We cover the costs that keep children out of class and train local teachers to make lessons better.

  • $30 buys school supplies for one child for a year
  • $120 covers a term of school fees
  • $500 trains a teacher
Support this program

Food and nutrition

Hungry children cannot learn. Our school meals and family food parcels keep children in class and families fed.

  • $10 provides school meals for a child for a month
  • $45 gives a family a food parcel
Support this program

Health care

Mobile clinics bring check-ups, vaccinations and basic treatment to villages far from a hospital.

  • $15 pays for a child's vaccinations
  • $60 funds a clinic day for a village
Support this program

Clean water

Wells and water filters protect children from disease and save hours spent fetching water.

  • $25 provides a household water filter
  • $900 builds a village well
Support this program
