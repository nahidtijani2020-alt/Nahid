About Hopebridge

We started in 2017 with one classroom and a group of volunteers. Today we support thousands of children across the region.

Our story

Hopebridge began when a group of teachers noticed that many bright students were leaving school because their families could not afford fees or lunch. We raised funds for the first ten pupils. Word spread, neighbours joined in, and the work grew village by village.

We stay small on purpose. Local staff run each program, so decisions are made by people who know the families.

Our mission

To remove the barriers that keep children out of school and out of good health.

Our vision

A region where every child can learn, grow and plan a future.

What we stand for

Transparency

We publish how funds are used every year, so donors can see the impact.

Dignity

We work with families, not for them. Communities help choose our priorities.

Lasting change

We invest in teachers, clinics and wells that keep serving long after we leave.
