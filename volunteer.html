Volunteer with Hopebridge

Your time can help children learn, families feel supported and communities grow stronger.

Why volunteer?

Our volunteers are the heart of our community work. From mentoring students to helping at school feeding programs, every hour supports real, local change.

Whether you are available for a few hours a month or a few days a week, there is a role that matches your skills and schedule.

Volunteer impact

We organize classroom support, community events, outreach drives, food distribution, and outreach for mothers and caregivers.

Many volunteers also help with fundraising, storytelling, admin support and local partnership building.

Ways to help

Mentoring

Support children with reading, homework help and confidence-building activities after school.

Outreach

Join mobile health visits, school support days and family resource events in local villages.

Community support

Assist with logistics, food distribution, childcare at events and local communication efforts.

What we are looking for

  • People with compassion, reliability and a willingness to learn
  • Helpers for school, outreach and community events
  • Skilled volunteers in communications, fundraising and administration
  • Local advocates who can connect families with support services

Ready to join us?

We welcome volunteers from all backgrounds. If you want to support the mission, we would love to hear from you.
